About Me

Hi, I'm Nupur Sharma

I'm a certified nutritionist and sports dietitian, working with elite athletes, corporate teams, and individual clients to build sustainable nutrition strategies. With an MSc in Nutrition & Dietetics and hands-on experience across clinical, sports, and corporate wellness settings, I combine science with practicality.

My approach is holistic and data-led. I look at your lifestyle, goals, medical history, and even DNA-based markers when relevant — to design a plan built around your life, not a template from the internet. Whether you want to lose weight, manage PCOS or diabetes, improve athletic performance, or simply feel more energetic, the right plan is the one you can actually follow.
